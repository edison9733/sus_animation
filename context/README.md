**Facts the film relies on**
- **Project:** a low-cost kit that upgrades existing pit latrines to survive floods and droughts.
- **Pilot area:** Jikawo, Wantawo and Lare woredas, plus one refugee camp.
- **Pilot size:** 500 households (about 2,900 people), at an estimated USD 540k (range 333k–818k).
- **Toilet zones:** zone A is raised or sealed (211k people), zone B a raised slab over a lined pit (110k), zone C a lined pit on clay (275k), and zone D steep ground (36k).
