# Text to read aloud for ElevenLabs instant voice cloning (~1.5 min)

Record in a quiet room, phone about a hand's width from your mouth, normal pace. Save as MP3 (well under 10 MB).

Hi, my name is Edison, and I study electronic and computer engineering. Today I want to talk about something simple that matters a lot: a safe toilet and clean drinking water.

In many villages, families use a pit latrine, with a shallow well just a few steps away. Most of the time, that works. But when a flood comes, water rises from above and from below. The pit overflows, and waste can reach the well.

So what can a family actually do? Our idea is called FloodSafe. We don't replace the whole system. We upgrade the latrine people already have: lined walls, a raised slab, a sliding lid and a vent pipe.

A small gauge shows green, yellow or red. Green means use it as usual. Yellow means treat your drinking water. Red means seal the lid and use the emergency pack.

Is it perfect? No. Is it cheap, local and practical? We think so. Next, we want to test it for two seasons, wet and dry, with a local partner. Thanks for listening!
