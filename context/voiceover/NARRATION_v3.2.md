## Checks on the words

- **Every number** above is on screen in v3.2 and comes from the data pack. See the figure table in `context/HANDOFF_README.md`.
- **"More than the six other years put together":** 2022 had 185,200 displaced. 2019, 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024 and 2025 together had 143,306 (`gambella_flood_drought_yearly.csv`).
- **"More than half the budget":** toilet units (zones A and B) are USD 291,000 of the USD 540,280 mid estimate, 54% (`budget_summary.csv`).
- **Women and girls at risk at night:** this is from studies across sub-Saharan Africa and India in `sanitation_violence_literature.csv`, not a Gambela survey. Say "at risk", not a number.
- **"Far above Ethiopia's 27%":** DHS 2024: Gambela 46.9%, Ethiopia 27.2% of the population (`gambella_economy_sanitation.csv`).

## Recording tips

- **Face camera:** put it in the **bottom-right corner**, below the window and right of the captions. On a 1920 × 1080 recording, keep it inside x 1700–1910 and y 850–1070 (about 210 × 220 px). That strip is clear in every scene; anything wider covers the last letters of the kit caption ("…already have") and the pilot title ("…Gambella"). Keep the left panel (the charts) and the top-right (the gauges) uncovered.
- **Captions:** the big caption under the window repeats the key line of each scene, so the narration doesn't need to read it out word for word.
- **Two voices:** Narrator A speaks while the video plays, and Narrator B explains during the pauses. One person can do both.
- **Sound:** turn the video's music down to about 20% while speaking, or mute it.
